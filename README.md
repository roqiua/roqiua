### 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer and Control Systems Engineering graduate from Mansoura University and an ITI graduate with a background in Business Intelligence.

I have **2+ years of experience in Database Development and Business Documentation**, working with SQL Server, stored procedures, business requirements, and use-case documentation.

My experience combines database development with an understanding of business processes and data requirements. I'm currently expanding my skills in **Data Engineering, ETL development, and Data Warehousing**, while continuing to develop my data analysis and visualization skills.

I enjoy solving data-related problems, designing reliable data workflows, and continuously learning new technologies.
